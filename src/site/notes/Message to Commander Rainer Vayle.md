# Original text

> "Commander Vayle,
> 
> I've done as you requested, the town is ready for your forces to arrive. By this time next month, you'll doubtlessly be at Khazaddun receiving a medal for your service here, and I'll finally get the respect I so rightfully deserve. 
> 
> Glory to Anbuur,
> Landers Amfrey
> 
> P.S. Bring some manpower, I doubt you'll meet any resistance from the populace—I've made sure of that—but there's still the matter of those "loose ends" we'll want to clean up by the lake."
