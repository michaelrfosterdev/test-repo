When approaching or at death's door, player characters are able to tap into previously inaccessible wellsprings of power and will to turn the tides of battle. At various stages of hurt/dying, players may access the following beneficial features on their turn if they meet the requirements.
## Desperate Exertion

**When a player is bloodied** (i.e., they have 50% or less of their maximum hit points), they can choose to take one or more automatic Death Saving Throw failures to gain special bonuses or take extra actions. A player with 3 Death Saving Throw failures will remain alive so long as they have hit points, but if they lose all of their hit points and are knocked Unconscious, they are immediately killed. Death Saving Throw failures gained in this way remain until finishing a Short Rest. 
### 1 failure
- Take an extra Dash and Disengage action.
- +5 bonus to a d20 test.
- Reroll a d20 test.
### 2 failures
- Maximize the damage dice of an attack roll.
### 3 failures
- Take an extra Attack or Magic action.
- Regain a spell slot.
## Will of the Dying

**When a player is downed and dying**, they still have options to help sway the tide of battle. After a player rolls a death saving throw, they can perform one of the following moves:

1. **If the character has no death saving throw failures**, they may choose to instantly stabilize. Note: this means they will no longer roll death saving throws on subsequent turns, and can no longer activate the Actions of the Dying feature.
2. A character may choose any creature within 50ft that can see them and give them Heroic Inspiration.
