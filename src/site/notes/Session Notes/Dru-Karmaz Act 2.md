# Session Description
The party continues their investigation surrounding the demon attacks on [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Dru-Karmaz\|Dru-Karmaz]], making various discoveries and moving closer to uncovering exactly what lies at the FINISH THIS  

The session extends from October 26th-27th, 3713 ASC.
# Some Time Earlier...
Before the party met in [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Kithraia\|Kithraia]], a few of them were specifically requested to join the mission. 
## Briefing With the Boundless
[[Players/Zazel Amray\|Zazel]] speaks with [[Organizations/Band of the Boundless/Alfred Amray\|Alfred Amray]] in the [[Organizations/Band of the Boundless/Band of the Boundless\|Band of the Boundless]]'s camp outside of [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Kithraia\|Kithraia]]. During this conversation, Alfred asks that Zazel investigate the demonic rumors in [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Dru-Karmaz\|Dru-Karmaz]]. He's worried that with everyone distracted by the brewing war, the cults of [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]] are going to make their move soon.

[[Organizations/Band of the Boundless/Alfred Amray\|Alfred]] warns [[Players/Zazel Amray\|Zazel]] that, even though one might suspect the [[Organizations/Cults/Servants of the Writhing Dark\|Servants of the Writhing Dark]] to be responsible for the demon in [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Dru-Karmaz\|Dru-Karmaz]], all cults strike deals for power with beings blasphemed by the [[Church of the Divine Aspects\|Church]].
## Orders from [[Baronetess Pakris\|Baronetess Pakris]]
[[Players/Knight Eden\|Knight Eden]] speaks with [[Baronetess Pakris\|Baronetess Vothil Pakris]] in [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Pavarax's Frosthold/Pavarax's Frosthold\|Pavarax's Frosthold]]—one of her superiors in the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Pavarax's Frosthold/Knightly Order of the World Tree\|Knightly Order of the World Tree]]—about a request from the [[Organizations/Honored Watch\|Honored Watch]]. Due to the rising tensions between [[Nations/Jkaverstelle\|Jkaverstelle]] and [[Nations/Anbuur\|Anbuur]], the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Pavarax's Frosthold/Knightly Order of the World Tree\|Order]] has been requested to send reinforcements to [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Kithraia\|Kithraia]], as well as attend a meeting with the [[King's Assembly\|King's Assembly]] regarding the conflict sometime before the end of the year.

Leaving her students under the care of [[Baronetess Pakris\|Baronetess Pakris]], [[Players/Knight Eden\|Knight Eden]] prepares to journey to [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Kithraia\|Kithraia]] early and support the [[Organizations/Honored Watch\|Honored Watch]] in whatever mission they have for her. She'll soon be followed by Knights Sir [[Aggnare Brightdelver\|Aggnare Brightdelver]] and Dame [[Lillian Becker\|Lillian Becker]]—who served under [[Players/Knight Eden\|Knight Eden]] as squires years prior—then finally, [[Baronetess Pakris\|Baronetess Pakris]] will join the three of them in [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Kithraia\|Kithraia]] after she concludes her business in [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Pavarax's Frosthold/Pavarax's Frosthold\|Pavarax's Frosthold]]. 
# Investigations Around Town
The party investigates various leads in and around [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Dru-Karmaz\|Dru-Karmaz]], questioning a few key players in this conflict and searching the two buildings destroyed on the first night of the demon's attack. 
## The Amfrey Estate
The party arrives at [[Session Planning/1. Dru-Karmaz/Landers Amfrey\|Landers Amfrey]]'s estate southwest of [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Dru-Karmaz\|Dru-Karmaz]]—a beautiful brick manor unlike any other building in town. After being led inside by [[Aletta Joppen\|Aletta Joppen]], a maid working at the estate, the party meets with Landers.

Discussing the demon, [[Session Planning/1. Dru-Karmaz/Landers Amfrey\|Landers]] laments the destruction around town, but ensures the party that he has the situation well handled. According to him, the clerics of the [[Organizations/Ashers/Ashers\|Ashers]] are more than capable of fending off the beast with wards for the time being. Then, in a number of days, he described how a larger shipment of [[Creatures/Griffon\|Griffon]] blood is arriving, enough to slay the demon once and for all.

When [[Players/Xander Amfrey\|Xander]] asks [[Session Planning/1. Dru-Karmaz/Landers Amfrey\|Landers]] about the dip in lumber shipments out of [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Dru-Karmaz\|Dru-Karmaz]] the last few months, Landers describes how recent attempts to unionize by local employees have significantly hurt production.

[[Session Planning/1. Dru-Karmaz/Landers Amfrey\|Landers]] then asks the party to investigate the recent disappearance of one of his messengers—[[Guiles\|Guiles]]. With the deaths of Sheriff [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Urthgar Vra-Eklund\|Urthgar Vra-Eklund]] and Deputy [[Bronson\|Bronson]], no one has been available to find out what happened to the missing courier. 
## The Asher Camp
The party finds the [[Organizations/Ashers/Ashers\|Asher]] camp on the northeastern beach of [[Kuku-Karmaz\|Kuku-Karmaz]]. They see a number of large wooden tables set out along the beach—each with a roast pig on top—a few racks with wolf-pelts, and the remnants of a large bonfire. A nearby cave has its entrance covered by a makeshift wall and door, hiding additional parts of the camp.

The leader of the [[Organizations/Ashers/Ashers\|Ashers]], [[Organizations/Ashers/Angor\|Angor]], greets the party. He apologizes for the actions of [[Organizations/Ashers/Bruly Mensif\|Bruly]] and [[Organizations/Ashers/Fesh Ploughly\|Fesh]] at the [[Drowsy Maid\|Drowsy Maid]], and assures them that those two won't go near the brothel again. The party buys another vial of [[Creatures/Griffon\|Griffon]] blood, and sit down to eat some pork with the gang. [[Organizations/Ashers/Bruly Mensif\|Bruly]] seems especially on edge when the party speaks with him, seeming even more scared than when the party threatened his life earlier. 
## Asking Around Town
[[Players/Zazel Amray\|Zazel]] tracks down an old acquaintance from when the [[Organizations/Band of the Boundless/Band of the Boundless\|Band of the Boundless]] had visited [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Dru-Karmaz\|Dru-Karmaz]] years back, the blacksmith [[Klaus Barend\|Klaus Barend]]. Klaus seemed happy to see Zazel, but frustrated with the circumstances of the town; he suspects [[Session Planning/1. Dru-Karmaz/Landers Amfrey\|Landers]] summoned the demon, saying that it was too convenient that both the Sheriff's office and the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Red March Tavern\|Red March Tavern]] were destroyed in the first night. But, after [[Players/Zazel Amray\|Zazel]] asked why there were no weapons hung in the shop, [[Klaus Barend\|Klaus's]] attitude turned sour and he quickly closed up shop.

[[Players/Eleanor Ego\|Ego]] followed up with [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Gruldrum Runeshaper\|Gruldrum Runeshaper]] at the [[Drowsy Maid\|Drowsy Maid]], asking after [[Guiles\|Guiles]]. According to Gruldrum, the evening before the first demon attack Guiles was causing his usual trouble at the brothel when sheriff [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Urthgar Vra-Eklund\|Vra-Eklund]] escorted him out of the building. 
## The Sheriff's Office
[[Players/Eleanor Ego\|Ego]] and [[Players/Xander Amfrey\|Xander]] search the destroyed sheriff's office for clues. Xander find an incident report signed by Deputy [[Bronson\|Bronson]] describing how [[Guiles\|Guiles]] had been making trouble at the [[Drowsy Maid\|Drowsy Maid]] and was brought to the station to sober up in the cell until morning. Ego searches Sheriff [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Urthgar Vra-Eklund\|Vra-Eklund's]] desk, and find a crumpled message from [[Session Planning/1. Dru-Karmaz/Landers Amfrey\|Landers]] in the nearby wastebin, reading "Do your job and deal with the union! They are going to kill me!" The two then investigate the cell in the back of the station, finding the wall behind the bars collapsed and a large bloodstain behind the still-closed cell door. They also find a leather satchel with the letters "[[Amfrey Lumber Company\|ALC]]" branded on its front; inside which they find a number of shipment papers, and an [[Message to Commander Rainer Vayle\|encoded message]].
## The Red March Tavern
[[Players/Knight Eden\|Knight Eden]] and [[Players/Magnolia\|Magnolia]] search the destroyed [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Dru-Karmaz/Red March Tavern\|Red March Tavern]] for clues. The barn like building is annihilated, its large walls crumpled inward, making the building strenuous to traverse. However, Magnolia notices a shallow storage space hidden behind the bar, exposed due to a falling keg from the destroyed loft above; inside the alcove are four bundles of swords, war hammers, and axes, each bundle containing around a dozen weapons each.  
# Evening at the Amfrey Estate
After a day of searching, the party retires to the [[Amfrey\|Amfrey]] estate southwest of town. While there, they continue their investigation late into the night.
## Conversing With the Cook
Ego speaks with the cook at the [[Amfrey\|Amfrey]] estate—[[Harmen Kessler\|Harmen Kessler]]—to follow up on a feeling of unease she noticed during their initial visit to the mansion. Since [[Players/Eleanor Ego\|Ego]] had actually briefly  taught his sister—[[Cor De Kessler\|Cor De Kessler]]—at [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Roseström University/Roseström University\|Roseström University]], they quickly establish a rapport. Harmen noticed that whenever someone from the [[Organizations/Ashers/Ashers\|Ashers]] stops by with [[Creatures/Griffon\|Griffon]] blood, [[Session Planning/1. Dru-Karmaz/Landers Amfrey\|Landers]] is perfectly cordial during the exchange, but seems disgusted as soon as they leave; Harmen worries that Landers will lose his composure one of these days and be denied the blood keeping the manor safe. 
## Landers' Study
After dark, [[Players/Xander Amfrey\|Xander]] sneaks into a locked room they saw [[Session Planning/1. Dru-Karmaz/Landers Amfrey\|Landers]] leave from earlier that evening; inside, he finds Landers' personal study. Discovering a false back to the main desk drawer, Xander finds the cipher-key for decoding the letter the party had found earlier in the Sheriff's office. After decoding the document, it appears to be a [[Message to Commander Rainer Vayle\|message]] sent by Landers to Commander [[Rainer Vayle\|Rainer Vayle]], a high-ranking [[Nations/Anbuur\|Anbuuri]] military officer.

[[Players/Zazel Amray\|Zazel]] also investigates the room, and searching the bookshelves and cabinets for anything related to demonology, he discovers a hidden alcove under the floorboards at the corner of the room. In this stash, Zazel finds a research paper written by [[Players/Eleanor Ego\|Ego]] on materials required for demonic rituals, and a set of those exact same components (a vial of black salt, incents, and precious gems). After analyzing the materials, [[Players/Eleanor Ego\|Ego]] confirms that they seem to be legitimate, but notes that it's strange that they seem entirely unused.
