# Basic Information

There are a number of cults active in [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]], each with their own characteristics and goals, and each condemned by the [[Church of the Divine Aspects\|Church of the Divine Aspects]]. The [[Church of the Divine Aspects\|Church]] warns that these cults serve un-holy powers opposing the five remaining [[The World/Divine Aspects\|Divine Aspects]], and they all seek to dismantle the good societies of [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]]. With dangerous warlocks and artificers filling their ranks, these cults are not to be taken lightly. Over the last few years, cult activity has noticibly grown throughout [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]], with new sightings of their ilk reported each month.  
# Cults of [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]]

## [[Cult of the Stars\|Cult of the Stars]]

The [[Cult of the Stars\|Cult of the Stars]] is an organization marked by raving madness, intellectual brilliance, and terrifying foresight. Founded by the [[Mad Prophet\|Mad Prophet]] in the [[The Ages/Third Age\|Third Age]], this cult follows the nonsensical teachings of their central text—the [[Book of the Stars\|Book of the Stars]]. Despite the apparent insanity of many of its members, the [[Cult of the Stars\|Cult of the Stars]] leverages unnatural powers of divination to enigmatically achieve their goals over long stretches of seemingly un-related missions. 

Along with its many heretical beliefs, the [[Cult of the Stars\|Cult of the Stars]] is infamous for creating and coveting "stellar-scopes"—long range spyglasses allowing individuals to peer into the cosmos. The [[Church of the Divine Aspects\|Church of the Divine Aspects]] has outlawed the use of these telescopes, warning that [[The World/Divine Aspects/Strozkus\|Strozkus]]—god of the stars—does not tolerate the unworthy gazing into his domain.

In the current year 3713 ASC, recent activity in ancient [[The Ages/Third Age\|Third]] and [[The Ages/Second Age\|Second Age]] ruins and isolated libraries imply that the [[Cult of the Stars\|Cult of the Stars]] has been searching for some knowledge, but it's unknown what an organization with such potent divination could be searching for.
## [[Draakic Arcanum\|Draakic Arcanum]]

The [[Draakic Arcanum\|Draakic Arcanum]] is an organization that reveres [[Creatures/Dragons\|dragons]] and aims to see the [[Draconic Dynasty\|Draconic Dynasty]] of the [[The Ages/Second Age\|Second Age]] reestablished to rule over [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]]. Members of this cult refer to the toppling of the [[Draconic Dynasty\|Draconic Dynasty]] as the "Greatest of All Sins", and their efforts center around reversing this event and restoring dragons to their rightful station ruling [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]]. But despite the avid fealty of its members and the draconic symbols central to the cult, no dragon has revealed itself at the center of the organization, leading some to believe that the [[Draakic Arcanum\|Draakic Arcanum]] acts entirely independently of their would-be rulers. 

Compared to the other cults active on the continent, the [[Draakic Arcanum\|Draakic Arcanum]] is the most traditionally militant of the group, with powerful martials and war-sorcerers among its ranks. Because of this, bands of cult members often disguise as mercenaries-for-hire, taking on jobs to expand their influence and accumulate money for the organization.

In the current year 3713 ASC, recent activity in the mountainous regions of [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]] imply that the [[Draakic Arcanum\|Draakic Arcanum]] has been accumulating metal and weapons, but it's unknown what conflict they're preparing for.
## [[Children of the Weave\|Children of the Weave]]

The [[Children of the Weave\|Children of the Weave]] is an organization that worships [[Kethryllia\|Kethryllia]]—the dead goddess of magic. People had worshiped [[Kethryllia\|Kethryllia]] well before the end of the [[The Ages/Third Age\|Third Age]], so after the goddess' apparent death in [[Cities and Towns/Linthir'Aprimos\|Linthir'Aprimos]], a number of her followers began working to try reviving their deity. Many [[Elves\|Elves]] support this cult, hoping that in restoring their goddess, their species may avoid extinction in the following millennia.  

The [[Church of the Divine Aspects\|Church of the Divine Aspects]] condemns the [[Children of the Weave\|Children of the Weave]], claiming that any power they can siphon off their goddess' corpse can only corrupt them. Regardless of how they source their powers, this cult enjoys potent magical abilities reminiscent of those used by the [[Nations/Linthirian Empire\|Linthirian Empire]] to conquer [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]] in the [[The Ages/Third Age\|Third Age]]. In addition, the [[Children of the Weave\|Children of the Weave]] are unique among these cults, as they reportedly have a number of clerics and paladins in their ranks—a claim vehemently denied by the [[Church of the Divine Aspects\|Church]] 

In the current year 3713 ASC, recent activity near sealed [[Cities and Towns/Linthir'Aprimos\|Linthir'Aprimos]] and other major cities of the [[The Ages/Third Age\|Third Age]] imply that the [[Children of the Weave\|Children of the Weave]] are seeking hidden [[Elven\|Elven]] wisdom, but it's unclear if they've made any progress in their ultimate goal of reviving their goddess.  
## [[Servants of the Writhing Dark\|Servants of the Writhing Dark]]

The [[Servants of the Writhing Dark\|Servants of the Writhing Dark]] is an organization that serves the [[demons\|demons]] of [[The World/Dor'Daedeloth\|Dor'Daedeloth]]. Not much is known about their grand goals, but this cult mainly busies itself with the dismantling of [[Church of the Divine Aspects\|Church]] institutions, and the accumulation of mortal souls for their dark lords. The large number of powerful warlocks and artificers in this cult speaks to the power on offer for anyone willing to sell their soul and join this shadowy cause.

Unsurprisingly, the [[Church of the Divine Aspects\|Church of the Divine Aspects]] vilifies the [[Servants of the Writhing Dark\|Servants of the Writhing Dark]] above practically all other cults. Mortal sacrifices, demonic rituals, desecration of holy institutions—this cult appears as the most malicious even among its fellow organizations.

In the current year 3713 ASC, recent activity has been suspiciously quiet. One would think that the malicious history of this cult would see it leading the wave of increased cult activity in the last few years, but surprisingly, no. No gathering of forces, no rumors of secret meetings, no large-scale slaughter. The only actions attributed to the [[Servants of the Writhing Dark\|Servants of the Writhing Dark]] were a small number of rituals committed over the last few years spread sporadically throughout [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]], the only commonality between these events: each ritual involved the sacrifice of just a single [[Elf\|Elf]].
## [[Court of Eros\|Court of Eros]]

The [[Court of Eros\|Court of Eros]] is an organization that serves the [[Fey\|Fey]] of [[The World/Dor'Daedeloth\|Dor'Daedeloth]] and the high-house of the fey court: the [[House of Eros\|House of Eros]]. Members of the [[Court of Eros\|Court of Eros]] rarely join willingly, instead they find themselves on the wrong side of a fey bargain or tricked into forfeiting their agency. At the instruction of their [[Fey\|Fey]] patrons, members of the [[Court of Eros\|Court of Eros]] open [[Seam\|Seams]] between [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]] and their lands in [[The World/Dor'Daedeloth\|Dor'Daedeloth]], which act as portals for the [[Creatures/Mortal Races/Mortal Races\|mortal races]] to accidentally walk through and be set upon by conniving [[Fey\|Fey]]. 

The [[Court of Eros\|Court of Eros]] is typically non-confrontational in [[The World/Dor'Balar\|Dor'Balar]], preferring to secretly open [[Seam\|Seams]] to the lands of [[House of Eros\|Eros]] and waiting for mortals to wander in. However, this apparent passivity obscures the true horror of the [[Fey\|Fey]], as those unlucky enough to find themselves on the other side of a [[Seam\|Seam]] rarely leave without losing a fundamental part of themselves.

In the current year 3713 ASC, recent activity creating [[Seam\|Seams]] in the swamps of east [[Nations/Jkaverstelle\|Jkaverstelle]], the [[Morkay Timberlands\|Morkay Timberlands]], and the mountain ranges of [[Nations/Anbuur\|Anbuur]] imply more aggressive movements, but it's unknown what spurred the [[Court of Eros\|Court of Eros]] to this uptick in behavior.
