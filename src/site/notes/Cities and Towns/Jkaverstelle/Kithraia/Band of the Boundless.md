# Basic Information

The [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Band of the Boundless\|Band of the Boundless]] is a respected squadron of soldiers-for-hire active in [[Nations/Jkaverstelle\|Jkaverstelle]]. Led by [[Alfred Amray\|Alfred Amray]]—a retired veteran of the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Honored Watch\|Honored Watch]]—this band is often hired by institutions around the Kingdom to assist in everything from bolstering border defenses to training new recruits. 

Despite [[Alfred Amray\|Alfred Amray]]'s prestige as a former member of the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Honored Watch\|Honored Watch]], the other members of the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Band of the Boundless\|Band of the Boundless]] are far less reputable; among its members are ex-knights, former criminals, and similar folks left behind by society. Despite this however, the Band is known throughout the Kingdom for its uncompromising effectiveness and impressive flexibility on the battlefield.
# People

## [[Alfred Amray\|Alfred Amray]]

[[Alfred Amray\|Alfred Amray]] is a [[Human\|Human]] man, and the no-nonsense leader of the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Band of the Boundless\|Band of the Boundless]] hardened by years of experience in the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Honored Watch\|Honored Watch]]. He became jaded during his time as a soldier, fiercely protecting those in his band, but holding a deep cynicism for everyone outside his fold. At 67 years old, Alfred is well past his prime, but through some combination of sheer willpower and spite he still puts his subordinates to shame in martial skill and physical endurance. 
## Grete of Galdeen

Grete of Galdeen is an [[Orc\|Orc]] woman, an ex-paladin of [[The World/Divine Aspects/Gudmunhil\|Gudmunhil]], and currently a soldier in the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Band of the Boundless\|Band of the Boundless]]. Although she no longer gains strength from her god, Grete is respected as the "unbreakable wall" of the squadron; in any fight—whether on the battlefield or in the mead hall—she's doubtlessly the last Band member to go down.  
## Carousel Keen

Carousel Keen is a [[Human\|Human]] man, an ex-member of the [[Spicers Guild\|Spicers Guild]], and currently a soldier in the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Band of the Boundless\|Band of the Boundless]]. Once drowning in debt on the shores of the [[Rosewood Portduchy\|Rosewood Portduchy]], Carousel originally joined the Bound to escape the local loan sharks. His hands as quick as his wit, Carousel currently manages the finances of the Bound and specializes in solo missions into enemy territories.
## Lady Aurora

Lady Aurora is a [[Half-Dwarf\|Half-Dwarf]] woman, an ex-knight of House [[Thjorvidson\|Thjorvidson]], and currently a soldier in the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Band of the Boundless\|Band of the Boundless]]. Having achieved knighthood under a [[Nations/Jkaverstelle\|Great House]], Lady Aurora still maintains land in the [[Thjorvidson Lowduchy\|Thjorvidson Lowduchy]], but left her service for unknown reasons. Her formal training and levelled demeanor afford her a tactical mind rivaling even [[Alfred Amray\|Alfred's]]. By keeping her head even in the midst of battle, Lady Aurora efficiently assesses the field and delivers concise orders to her allies, ensuring the Band maintains its flexible and deadly reputation.
