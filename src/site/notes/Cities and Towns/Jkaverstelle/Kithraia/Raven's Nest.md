# Basic Information

The [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Raven's Nest\|Raven's Nest]] is an orphanage in the Midnight District of [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Kithraia\|Kithraia]]. Among the other orphanages in the city, it's perfectly average; but with the money given by the city, although they can afford few luxuries, the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Raven's Nest\|Raven's Nest]] is funded enough to keep themselves comfortable. 

None of the children go to bed hungry, but he meals are bland and repetitive. They have enough clothes and blankets to keep warm during the winter, but most of them are secondhand, passed down as children grow out of them. Basic education is provided by the caretakers, but older children are expected to assist with chores until they're equipped enough to find work outside the orphanage. 

Still, despite their average means, the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Raven's Nest\|Raven's Nest]] and orphanages like it provide a pivotal service keeping the children of [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Kithraia\|Kithraia]] housed, fed, and cared for.    

# People

## Aria Requi

Aria Requi is a half-orc woman caretaker at the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Raven's Nest\|Raven's Nest]] orphanage. Aria is in charge of cooking and resource acquisition in the orphanage, and is known for sneaking children small sweets on their birthday.
## Sister Magdalene

Sister Magdalene is a human woman caretaker at the [[Cities and Towns/Jkaverstelle/Kithraia/Raven's Nest\|Raven's Nest]] orphanage. Sister Magdalene is in charge of teaching the children, choir practice, and running the weekly sermons in the nearby church. She's known as one of the more strict caretakers at the orphanage and seems to take her job extremely seriously.
